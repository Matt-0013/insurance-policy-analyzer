## Scoring weights

| rule | weight | what it rewards | evidence in the corpus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `clean` | +2 | no exclusion evidence in the sentence, preamble, previous chunk or heading | ICICI p13 maternity: the IRDAI code sits in the heading, so a body-only check scored a carve-out as a clean statement |
| `definition` | +2 | the owning sentence defines the term ("means") | Niva p4 "Base Sum Insured means the amount stated in the Policy Schedule" beats an incidental mention elsewhere |
| `scheduled` | +1 | the chunk defers to the Policy Schedule or the Policy Certificate | ICICI p7 "amount specified in the Policy Certificate"; all three wordings defer quantities, only the brochure prints them |
| `quantity` | +1 | the chunk carries an amount, percentage or duration | Star p3 "Upto 1% of Sum Insured per day" against ICICI's 205,113 characters with no per-day figure |
| `comma_list` | -1 | three or more commas: a list of things, not a clause | Care p28 "Deductible, Non-payable claims, Co-payments, excess claim amount" outscored the real co-payment clause at p25 before this penalty existed |
| `section_code` | +4 | an IRDAI exclusion code in the section heading | Niva p12 "XVIII. Maternity Expenses (Code-Excl18)"; ICICI p13 "3. Maternity (Code - Excl 18)" |
| `coverage_cue` | +5 | a clean sentence that explicitly grants cover | Star p1 "providing cover ... comprehensive maternity-related benefits", in a brochure that also lists Excl 18 for the base indemnity |

The state of a cell comes from the highest-scoring candidate, not from the
first mention in document order. That single change is what turned both a
run of 30 false exclusions and a run of 41 false statements into one
consistent matrix.

| state | decision |
| --- | --- |
| `excluded` | the winning candidate carries exclusion evidence |
| `scheduled` | the winner defers to the schedule and states no number |
| `stated` | otherwise |
| `not stated` | no alias matched in a document proven complete |

## Evidence windows

| window | chars | what it reads | evidence in the corpus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `preamble_chars` | 250 | text before the match inside the same chunk | ICICI p34 "No benefit payable in any respect of any insured person for: a. ... b. Pregnancy" - the header governs the list that follows it |
| `next_chars` | 220 | text after the match, past the sentence end | ICICI Excl 01 "until the expiry of / 36 months of continuous coverage" - a PDF line break splits the duration into the next sentence |
| `tail_chars` | 250 | tail of the previous chunk | ICICI p8 "We shall not be liable ... in respect of Prehospitalization Medical Expenses" - the governing line is one chunk above the item |
| `code_radius` | 150 | characters either side of the match | Star p15 "a medical practitioner as part of hospitalization claim or day care procedure. Code-Excl 14" - the code follows the sentence |

Exclusion evidence is read narrowest first: the sentence owning the term,
then these windows. A chunk-wide window (plus both neighbours) produced 30
false exclusions; a sentence-only window produced 41 false statements. The
ladder exists because the true scope of a clause sits between those two.
